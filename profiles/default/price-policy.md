# Default Price Policy

- Price tables are adapter data, not Core rules.
- Always state the date and source of a price snapshot.
- Report low and high ranges when cache-hit assumptions are uncertain.
- Separate cached input, uncached input and output tokens.
- Stop or re-plan when the estimated budget is exceeded by 50% without validation progress.

Load `adapters/model-routing/deepseek-cc-switch` when exact DeepSeek pricing or CC Switch behavior is required.
